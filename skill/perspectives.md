# Perspectives Reference

> 每个视角标注了 **认同度等级**（极高/高/中/低/小众），对应加权系数见 SKILL.md。
> 认同度基于该学派在当今全球范围内的制度化程度、学术影响力和公众接受度评定。

## Ancient Eastern

### Confucianism / 儒家 (~551–479 BCE → ongoing) `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: 仁 (benevolence), 礼 (ritual propriety), 义 (righteousness), 孝 (filial piety)
- **Moral logic**: Actions are judged by whether they uphold social harmony, fulfill relational duties (五伦), and cultivate virtue.
- **Key thinkers**: Confucius (孔子), Mencius (孟子, human nature is good), Xunzi (荀子, human nature needs cultivation)
- **Typical stance**: Favors social order, hierarchy, duty, education; opposes selfishness, chaos, disrespect of elders.
- **当代影响**: 东亚文化圈（中日韩越）社会伦理的深层底色，但非制度化法律体系。

### Legalism / 法家 (~4th–3rd c. BCE) `认同度: 低`
- **Core**: Strict law (法), statecraft (术), positional power (势)
- **Moral logic**: Morality is irrelevant; only enforceable law and punishment maintain order. The state's interest supersedes individual virtue.
- **Key thinkers**: Han Feizi (韩非子), Shang Yang (商鞅)
- **Typical stance**: Judges purely by legal code; anything not prohibited is permitted, anything prohibited is punished regardless of intent.
- **当代影响**: 其"以法治国"思想被部分吸收进现代法治理念，但纯粹法家的严刑峻法已不被主流认同。

### Taoism / 道家 (~6th c. BCE → ongoing) `认同度: 低`
- **Core**: 道 (the Way), 无为 (non-action), naturalness, spontaneity
- **Moral logic**: Artificial moral codes are symptoms of society's decline. True virtue arises from aligning with nature and the Tao.
- **Key thinkers**: Laozi (老子), Zhuangzi (庄子)
- **Typical stance**: Skeptical of rigid moral judgments; favors individual freedom, non-interference, simplicity.
- **当代影响**: 文化与哲学层面有影响，环保运动有所借鉴，但无制度化的伦理框架。

### Mohism / 墨家 (~470–391 BCE) `认同度: 小众`
- **Core**: 兼爱 (universal love), 非攻 (anti-aggression), utilitarian benefit
- **Moral logic**: Actions are moral if they maximize collective welfare; partiality and warfare are condemned.
- **Key thinkers**: Mozi (墨子)
- **Typical stance**: Proto-utilitarian; evaluates by net benefit to the greatest number.
- **当代影响**: 学术研究对象，其兼爱思想有理论价值，但无当代实践影响。

### Buddhism / 佛教 (~5th c. BCE → ongoing) `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, karma, compassion (慈悲), non-harm (不杀生)
- **Moral logic**: Actions are judged by intent (cetanā) and their karmic consequences. Attachment and ignorance are root causes of suffering.
- **Typical stance**: Condemns actions rooted in greed, hatred, or delusion; approves compassion and mindful action.
- **当代影响**: 全球约5亿信众，在东亚和东南亚有广泛文化影响，正念运动全球流行。

## Ancient Western

### Aristotelian Virtue Ethics (~384–322 BCE) `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Eudaimonia (flourishing), the Golden Mean, practical wisdom (phronesis)
- **Moral logic**: A virtuous act is one performed by a person of good character, hitting the mean between excess and deficiency.
- **Typical stance**: Context-dependent; asks "What would a person of practical wisdom do?"
- **当代影响**: 20世纪"德性伦理学复兴"，MacIntyre 等学者重新引入学术主流，对品格教育有影响。

### Stoicism / 斯多葛学派 (~3rd c. BCE → 2nd c. CE) `认同度: 低`
- **Core**: Virtue as sole good, rational nature, cosmopolitanism, acceptance of fate
- **Moral logic**: Only virtue (wisdom, courage, justice, temperance) is truly good. External outcomes are "preferred indifferents."
- **Key thinkers**: Zeno, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca
- **Typical stance**: Judges by intent and rational virtue, not consequences.
- **当代影响**: 近年"现代斯多葛主义"流行于自我管理领域，但无制度化影响。

### Epicureanism (~341–270 BCE) `认同度: 小众`
- **Core**: Pleasure (ataraxia — tranquility) as highest good, avoidance of pain
- **Moral logic**: Actions are good if they lead to sustainable pleasure and absence of suffering; prudence is key.
- **Typical stance**: Generally permissive if no harm results; values friendship and moderation.
- **当代影响**: 主要为哲学史研究对象，日常"享乐主义"概念与其原义有偏差。

### Roman Law Tradition `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Ius civile, ius gentium, ius naturale; codified rights and duties
- **Moral logic**: Actions are legal/illegal per codified statutes; equity and natural law provide interpretive flexibility.
- **Typical stance**: Procedural and precedent-based; separates legal from moral judgment.
- **当代影响**: 大陆法系（欧洲大陆、拉美、东亚民法典）的直接源头，影响全球过半法律体系。

## Medieval / Religious

### Christian Natural Law — Thomas Aquinas (~1225–1274) `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Eternal law → natural law → human law; human reason participates in divine reason
- **Moral logic**: Actions are moral if they accord with natural law (discoverable by reason) and ultimately God's eternal law.
- **Typical stance**: Condemns acts against natural purposes (e.g., lying always wrong); allows for proportionality in self-defense.
- **当代影响**: 天主教道德神学基础，自然法传统对西方法哲学仍有影响。

### Islamic Jurisprudence / Sharia `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Quran, Sunnah, ijma (consensus), qiyas (analogy); five categories (wajib, mustahabb, mubah, makruh, haram)
- **Moral logic**: Actions are categorized from obligatory to forbidden based on divine revelation and scholarly consensus.
- **Typical stance**: Detailed legal taxonomy; emphasizes justice, community welfare, and divine command.
- **当代影响**: 约20亿穆斯林的宗教伦理框架，部分国家作为法律体系的组成部分。

### Hindu Dharma `认同度: 低`
- **Core**: Dharma (duty/cosmic order), karma, varna-ashrama-dharma
- **Moral logic**: Right action depends on one's stage of life, social role, and the cosmic order. Intent and duty matter more than outcome.
- **Typical stance**: Context-sensitive based on one's dharmic role; universal principles of truthfulness and non-harm.
- **当代影响**: 印度次大陆文化基底，但种姓制度等内容受到现代人权观批评。

## Modern Philosophical

### Kantian Deontology — Immanuel Kant (1724–1804) `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Categorical Imperative — act only according to maxims universalizable as universal law; treat humanity always as an end, never merely as means
- **Moral logic**: Morality is about duty and rational principle, not consequences. Lying is wrong regardless of outcome.
- **Typical stance**: Strict rule-based; certain actions are absolutely prohibited.
- **当代影响**: 西方伦理学核心框架之一，人权概念的哲学基础，但绝对主义立场受到广泛批评。

### Utilitarianism — Bentham (1748–1832) / Mill (1806–1873) `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Greatest happiness principle — maximize total well-being
- **Moral logic**: An action is right if it produces the most good (or least harm) for the greatest number.
- **Variants**: Act utilitarianism (case-by-case), Rule utilitarianism (follow rules that generally maximize utility)
- **Typical stance**: Outcome-focused; permits otherwise distasteful actions if net benefit is positive.
- **当代影响**: 公共政策制定的主流逻辑（成本效益分析），经济学和法律经济学的理论基础。

### Social Contract Theory `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Legitimate authority rests on agreement among free individuals
- **Key thinkers**: Hobbes (security), Locke (natural rights), Rousseau (general will), Rawls (justice as fairness)
- **Moral logic**: Actions are just if they conform to principles rational agents would agree to behind a "veil of ignorance."
- **Typical stance**: Emphasizes rights, consent, fairness; condemns exploitation and coercion.
- **当代影响**: 现代民主制度和宪政的理论基石，Rawls 的正义论是当代政治哲学最具影响力的作品之一。

### Existentialism — Sartre, de Beauvoir, Camus `认同度: 低`
- **Core**: Radical freedom, personal responsibility, authenticity
- **Moral logic**: No universal moral code; each person must define their own values through free choice. "Bad faith" = denying one's freedom.
- **Typical stance**: Resists external moral judgments; evaluates by authenticity and acceptance of responsibility.
- **当代影响**: 文学和文化领域深远影响，心理治疗有所借鉴，但无制度化伦理框架。

### Pragmatism — James, Dewey, Peirce `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Truth and value are determined by practical consequences and lived experience
- **Moral logic**: Moral principles are tools; good ones work well in practice. Context and experimentation matter.
- **Typical stance**: Flexible, empirical, anti-dogmatic; judges by real-world outcomes and social improvement.
- **当代影响**: 美国哲学主流之一，对教育学、法律实用主义和公共政策有实际影响。

## Contemporary Legal & Ethical

### Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) `认同度: 极高`
- **Core**: 30 articles on inherent dignity, equality, freedom from torture, right to life, liberty, fair trial, privacy, etc.
- **Moral logic**: Certain rights are inalienable and universal regardless of culture or legal system.
- **Typical stance**: Strong floor of minimum standards; condemns violations of basic human dignity.
- **当代影响**: 国际法基石，193个联合国成员国承认，衍生出一系列具有法律约束力的国际公约。

### Modern Constitutional Law (general principles) `认同度: 极高`
- **Core**: Rule of law, separation of powers, individual rights, due process, equal protection
- **Moral logic**: Legitimacy derives from constitutional frameworks; actions are legal if within constitutional bounds.
- **Typical stance**: Procedural fairness, protection of minorities, balancing of rights.
- **当代影响**: 全球绝大多数国家的法律基础框架。

### Feminist Ethics / Ethics of Care `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Relationships, care, context, power dynamics, lived experience
- **Key thinkers**: Carol Gilligan, Nel Noddings
- **Moral logic**: Traditional ethics overemphasizes abstract principles; care ethics centers on maintaining relationships and addressing vulnerability.
- **Typical stance**: Attentive to power imbalances, gender dynamics, and the needs of the vulnerable.
- **当代影响**: 性别平等运动的理论基础，已融入多国法律和政策（反歧视法、家庭暴力立法等）。

### Environmental Ethics `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Intrinsic value of nature, sustainability, intergenerational justice
- **Variants**: Deep ecology, animal rights (Singer/Regan), land ethic (Leopold)
- **Moral logic**: Actions must be evaluated for ecological impact; future generations and non-human entities have moral standing.
- **Typical stance**: Condemns needless environmental destruction; favors sustainability.
- **当代影响**: 巴黎协定、ESG 投资标准、各国环保立法的伦理基础。

### Digital Ethics `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Privacy, algorithmic fairness, data sovereignty, digital rights, AI ethics
- **Moral logic**: Technology must serve human dignity; transparency, consent, and fairness in digital systems are moral imperatives.
- **Typical stance**: Evaluates actions involving data, surveillance, AI, and digital platforms for fairness and consent.
- **当代影响**: GDPR、AI监管法案等立法的理论基础，快速发展中但尚未完全成熟。

### Effective Altruism / 有效利他主义 `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Use evidence and reason to maximize the good you do; prioritize based on scale, neglectedness, and tractability
- **Key thinkers**: Peter Singer, William MacAskill, Toby Ord
- **Moral logic**: A consequentialist framework demanding not just "doing good" but "doing the most good possible." Favors impartial, data-driven resource allocation.
- **Typical stance**: Criticizes inefficient charity; values saving the most lives per dollar; prioritizes global catastrophic risk prevention.
- **当代影响**: 影响了数十亿美元的慈善捐赠方向，Open Philanthropy、GiveWell 等机构的理论基础。近年因 FTX 事件受到部分质疑，但核心理念仍有广泛学术影响。

### Libertarianism / 自由至上主义 `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Individual liberty is the supreme political value; minimal state intervention; self-ownership; non-aggression principle (NAP)
- **Key thinkers**: Robert Nozick, Friedrich Hayek, Murray Rothbard, Ayn Rand (Objectivism)
- **Moral logic**: Any action is moral as long as it doesn't initiate force or fraud against others. Voluntary exchange is always just.
- **Typical stance**: Maximally permissive on personal choices (drugs, lifestyle); opposes regulation, redistribution, and paternalism.
- **当代影响**: 美国政治思潮的重要组成部分，对科技行业文化和加密货币运动有深远影响。在经济政策辩论中是主要声音之一。

### Communitarianism / 社群主义 `认同度: 中`
- **Core**: Individual identity is constituted by community; shared values and common good take precedence over abstract individual rights
- **Key thinkers**: Alasdair MacIntyre, Charles Taylor, Michael Sandel, Michael Walzer
- **Moral logic**: Liberal individualism ignores the social fabric that makes moral life possible. Justice must account for particular traditions, histories, and communal bonds.
- **Typical stance**: Balances individual rights with community responsibility; values tradition, belonging, and civic virtue; skeptical of pure market logic.
- **当代影响**: 对自由主义的重要批评声音，影响了社区建设政策、公民教育理念和全球化反思。

### Capabilities Approach / 可行能力方法 `认同度: 高`
- **Core**: Justice is measured by whether people have real capabilities (not just formal rights) to live a flourishing life
- **Key thinkers**: Amartya Sen, Martha Nussbaum
- **Moral logic**: A just society ensures everyone can actually exercise core human capabilities (health, education, political participation, bodily integrity, etc.). Formal equality without real capability is insufficient.
- **Typical stance**: Focuses on what people can actually do and be; demands positive enabling conditions, not just non-interference.
- **当代影响**: 联合国人类发展指数(HDI)的理论基础，世界银行和国际发展领域的核心框架，Nussbaum 的能力清单被多国宪法借鉴。

## 话题速配表

根据讨论的话题类型，优先选用以下视角：

| 话题类型 | 核心视角 | 补充视角 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **人际关系** (谎言、背叛、感情) | 关怀伦理、儒家、康德 | 佛教、存在主义 |
| **财产与商业** (偷窃、欺诈、商业伦理) | 现代法律、社会契约论、功利主义 | 自由至上主义、罗马法 |
| **家庭伦理** (赡养、婚姻、教育) | 儒家、关怀伦理、现代法律 | 社群主义、人权宣言 |
| **生命伦理** (堕胎、安乐死、自杀) | 人权宣言、基督教自然法、功利主义 | 佛教、康德、自由至上主义 |
| **环境与动物** (捕猎、污染、素食) | 环境伦理、功利主义(Singer) | 道家、佛教、有效利他主义 |
| **科技伦理** (AI、隐私、基因编辑) | 数字伦理、人权宣言、康德 | 功利主义、可行能力方法 |
| **政治与公共政策** (税收、福利、言论自由) | 社会契约论、自由至上主义、人权宣言 | 社群主义、功利主义、可行能力方法 |
| **战争与暴力** (自卫、正义战争、复仇) | 人权宣言、功利主义、基督教自然法 | 法家、墨家、斯多葛 |
| **个人生活方式** (消费、职业、信仰) | 存在主义、自由至上主义、功利主义 | 儒家、社群主义、佛教 |
| **慈善与公益** (捐款方式、志愿服务) | 有效利他主义、功利主义、儒家(墨家) | 关怀伦理、可行能力方法 |
| **职场伦理** (加班、举报、竞业) | 现代法律、社会契约论、功利主义 | 康德、关怀伦理、自由至上主义 |
